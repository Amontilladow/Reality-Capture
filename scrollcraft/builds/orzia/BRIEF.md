# ORZIA homepage: brief

Partly interviewed. The user supplied the palette image, the ORZIA mockup and four answers
(below). Everything else was authored from that material and is labeled **Authored**.

## User answers (verbatim choices)

- Target: "ORZIA website" (new standalone homepage, not a retheme of Reality-Capture).
- Fidelity: "Shop-first, scroll-enhanced": keep the mockup's store structure, add a layered
  hero and scroll moments.
- Assets: "Generate via kie.ai". **The API key request was then declined**, so no images were
  generated. Every product is an inline SVG render, built in the palette and swappable for photos.
- Brands: "Text names only": brand names in our type, no copied logos.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe**: Authored, from the mockup: soft, blossoming, rose-gold, a beauty counter at dusk.
   References: the palette card itself (six stacked pills on wine), a department-store
   beauty hall, a compact mirror opening.
2. **Journey (from the mockup's order)**: hero with jar and serum on a plinth among blossoms,
   then the four trust promises, then the four category doors, then the brands, then the close.
   Authored addition: one shade moment between categories and brands.
3. **Energy**: calm hero, calm categories, the one loud moment at the swatches, calm brands,
   settled close.
4. **Feeling**: see the curve. The one moment: the swatch stack fanning open.
5. **Never-seen-before (seed of the signature)**: Authored: the palette the user sent is the
   control. Pick a swatch and every product rendered on the page takes that shade.
6. **Range**: premium-minimal leaning soft-luxe, as the mockup shows. Not brutalist, not dense.
7. **World**: distinct scenes. A store, not a flight.
8. **Assets**: none usable (the mockup's renders are not licensed sources). Palette:
   #2D0A1E #6A1E3B #B94273 #E97DA6 #FFC9B9 #FFF3E6. Real claims from the mockup: free
   shipping on orders over AED 200, curated selection, authentic brands, easy returns.
   Brand names: Dynamogén, Vichy, Maybelline New York, Cosmo Natural, PLC Supplements, Kiss.

## Feeling curve

1. Hero: **calm allure**. The jar and serum rise on their plinth while a blossom branch slides
   past in front of them and the arch behind recedes.
2. Categories: **orientation**. Four doors wipe open in a quiet asymmetric grid. Deliberately
   the calmest act, so the peak lands.
3. Peak: **delight**. The ground drops to wine, the six-pill stack fans open, and touching a
   pill recolours every product on the page.
4. Brands: **trust**. Names they already buy slide past sideways, one at a time.
5. Close: **resolve**. One line, one button, and their chosen shade carried to the end.

## Peak

"You scroll and the colour swatches fan out like a compact, and when you tap one every
product on the site turns that shade." Lives in act 3, which has the largest span (2.6vh).

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you pick a lipstick shade and the whole shop dresses itself in it.

## Authored silence

None. The categories act is quiet on purpose but carries content all the way.
